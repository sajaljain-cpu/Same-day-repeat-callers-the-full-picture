Executive Summary

What we found, and what we're asking for

The situation

29.6% of inbound calls to the High & Low Pain queues are same-day repeats (HP 35.9%, LP 27.8%) against a <25% target. Low Pain is near target; High Pain is the battle.

The core insight

Repeats split into two families: issues never resolved (field/product — agent can't fix on call) and calls that failed (dropped, no voice). The biggest cause, 52%, is field — not the call centre.

The impact

The plan reaches <25% by week 2 and <20% by week 4, removing roughly 300 repeat calls a day — capacity that shifts from firefighting to first-call resolution.

Section 5 · Root Cause (from call audits)

Five root causes — what call recordings revealed

Listening to the top repeat calls grouped them into five distinct root causes. Critically, the largest (Internet/Field) is not a call-centre problem at all — agents handled those calls correctly, but the field had not resolved the issue.

A · Internet & Field Issues

Internet Supply Down + RDNI (Recharge done, internet not working)
57,685Total calls
26,125Repeat calls
45.3%Repeat rate
52%Of all repeats

Root cause

Field / dealer does not resolve in time and the customer gets no proactive update — so they call again and again just to check status.

Solution

Tighter L2-team coordination to cut dealer turnaround, plus automated SMS on ticket open & close (ID + ETA) so status-check calls stop.

📞 Customer Customer A called 3× in one day (from an alternate number). Ticket already open, no partner update. Agent handled it correctly each time — the field had simply not fixed it.
Owner: L2 Team + Field Ops + Tech (SMS trigger)  ·  Callback queue will NOT fix this

B · Disconnected / No Response

Disconnected by CX + No proper response by CX
9,613Total calls
7,852Repeat calls
81.7%Repeat rate
16%Of all repeats

Root cause

Call ended before resolution — dropped, voice too low, or customer unreachable. 82% call back the same day, many within 15 minutes.

Solution

New Ameyo callback queue: any call with talk-time ≤30 sec is auto-dialled back (~194/day). Handled by 5–6 dedicated agents.

⚡ 11,724 callbacks happened within 5 minutes of the previous call — these are dropped/failed calls, not genuine new issues.

Genuine agent / knowledge gaps — could not find the root cause of a UPI link failure, or did not explain plan expiry. Highest repeat rate of any service class.

Solution

Resolution scripts for the top 5 Wiom Net codes (cannot close until final step done) + targeted training with app-team backend access.

🛠 Customer Customer B — agent could not identify why the recharge link was not arriving; on the repeat call, plan expiry was poorly explained.

⚠ Cross-cutting · Measurement gap

Alternate-number calling

Audits found customers calling from alternate numbers — one made 3 calls from a different phone. Because the metric counts by phone number, these show as separate "unique" callers, so the true repeat rate is likely higher than reported. Switch the metric to Customer ID (available in the Ameyo report). Owner: Analytics / MIS.

Section 6 · Deep-dive Patterns

Three patterns that change the plan

Beyond the issue mix, the data shows when repeats spike, whether agents are the cause, and what changes between the first and second call. These shape where to focus — and where not to waste effort.

1 · Time of day — mornings resolve, afternoons repeat

Share of calls in each hour that are part of a same-day repeat. High Pain climbs from ~54% at 8 AM to ~70% by 4 PM. Put your strongest agents on morning High Pain slots.
High Pain Low Pain

2 · Not an agent-skill problem

All agents cluster in a narrow 61–68% band. If it were a coaching issue you'd see outliers. The problem is structural — agents can't fix a down line on the call. Coaching won't move this.

3 · What changes on the call-back

First call is often dropped (Incomplete 28%). On the second call the customer gets through, but the issue is now overwhelmingly Internet (60%) — still unresolved by the field.
